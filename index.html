Privacy Policy — My IPTV

Last updated: 24 November 2026

My IPTV ("the app") is a media player. It does not provide, host or sell any channels, movies or series. You use it with an IPTV subscription that you own and are authorised to use.

Data the app handles

  • Your IPTV login (server address, username, password). You type it in yourself. It is stored only on your device, encrypted with the Android Keystore, and sent only to the IPTV server you entered, in order to load and play your content.
  • Preferences and watch history (favourites, recently watched channels, resume positions, language and playback settings, parental PIN). Stored only on your device; the PIN is encrypted.
  • Optional banner link. If you add a Spotlight URL in Settings, the app downloads that file from the address you entered.

Data we collect

None. The app has no analytics, advertising SDK, crash-reporting service or account system, and the developer receives no data from your device.

Sharing

Nothing is shared with the developer or third parties. Network requests go only to the IPTV server and the optional URLs that you enter yourself.

Deleting your data

Choose Settings → Log out to remove your login, cached lists and watch history from the device, or uninstall the app.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Contact

Questions or requests: please use the developer e-mail address shown on the app's Google Play listing.
